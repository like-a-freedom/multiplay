# PRD: The Multiplication Table as a Short Game

**Status:** a family project · **Date:** September 29, 2026 · **Device:** iPhone 12 Pro Max · **User:** the son, 10 years old, 4th grade.

## 1. Task and boundaries

The son needs to memorize the multiplication table. We will build a simple Russian-language PWA: short game missions with cards, cumulative progress, and spaced review of difficult examples. After a successful cache the app works without a network, stores data on the phone, and requires no registration, no server, and no App Store publication. The application is built for one child, not for a market.

**Required:** cards, answer checking, review on different days, an understandable game progression. **Later, if needed:** manual accounting of entertainment time. A PWA cannot change system-level Screen Time restrictions on an iPhone.

## 2. Learning principle

A card first shows `7 × 8 = ?`; the child enters a number or presses «Не знаю». Only after an attempt does the answer appear. After an error or «Не знаю» one explanatory strategy is shown, for example `7 × 8 = 7 × 5 + 7 × 3 = 35 + 21`; for simple facts — a short repeated-addition hint in school language (such as «три раза по четыре»). The parent can use equal groups of objects separately to check understanding; card hints need no object diagrams. Several pre-written templates are used without a separate theory lesson. An error is not punished: the example returns in the following missions. Familiar facts are mixed with a few new ones. The review intervals for the first prototype are 1, 3, 7, and 14 days; this is a **product setting**, not a scientifically proven optimal schedule.

This approach draws on research about [retrieval practice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20951630/), [distributed practice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40564553/), and [teaching the multiplication table with less interference between similar facts](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36459276/). At the same time, cards primarily train fact recall: an [RCT with children who struggle in mathematics](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24295141/) found no significant between-group differences in applying facts to word problems. That is not proof that no transfer exists. First we will find out whether the son understands what multiplication means, and if needed we will show equal groups of objects as a prompt.

## 3. One game loop

1. The home screen shows one main action: **«Играть»**, **«Повторить»** when reviews are due, or **«Свободная практика»** in maintenance without due reviews. It shows the daily streak during the expedition, plus total XP, level, and opened achievement stars.
2. A mission contains up to **10 cards**: reviews plus up to two new facts, for which slots are reserved. Each card has one answer and immediate calm feedback.
3. On finish the child gets XP within the daily limit, sees a short congratulation, and their progress. They can stop for today or play one more mission.
4. The next day the app offers the difficult examples again. The «Карта звёзд» screen with achievements and separate marks for facts due for review is reachable from the home screen and from the mission finish.

**The first-version game rules** are inspired by [Duolingo's short lessons, XP and streaks](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo/) but do not copy its full feature set:

| Mechanic | Simple rule |
| --- | --- |
| **XP and level** | +10 XP for a completed mission with at least one entered answer, correct or wrong; level equals `1 + floor(XP / 100)`. XP does not measure knowledge. More than three missions a day are allowed, but extra missions earn no extra XP. |
| **Daily streak** | Until the expedition is finished, one completed lesson with at least one entered answer per local calendar day extends the streak. After a break the current streak starts over; the best result is kept. History and XP are preserved; a forgotten fact gets a «Пора повторить» mark. |
| **Regularity bonus** | On the 3rd, 7th, 14th, and 30th day of a continuous streak add +20 XP and a short congratulation. Each milestone pays once in the current streak; maintenance mode has no new streak bonuses. |
| **Knowledge map** | A fact earns a permanent star after two independent successes on different days at least 7 days apart with no error between them. A later error adds «Пора повторить» but never dims the star. The map does not block new lessons. |

This is the whole game economy. No coins, no shop, no lives, no leaderboards, no timer, and no penalties for errors. The streak and XP help the child come back; the map separately shows achievements and the need for review. A research overview of gamification in education among older students ([pubmed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37636393/)) describes a possible short-term motivation boost and a risk of the effect fading over time; transfer to a ten-year-old is unproven, and the rules above are a hypothesis for this son, not a confirmed way to improve memory. 🔶 **Assumption:** 10 cards and these streak milestones suit him; check during trial sessions.

**How rewards are presented:** on mission finish name XP for practice, total XP and level, then the number of stars out of 66 with a «Карта звёзд» button. XP only raises the level; they never convert into stars. A star belongs to one specific expression and can open right after an answer, before mission finish; at that moment show «Новая звезда открыта на карте!». On the map an expandable list names each expression and its status, and «Пора повторить» is shown separately. A mission with entered wrong answers still earns effort XP but no star. A mission made entirely of «Не знаю» earns no XP and does not extend the streak or consume a daily reward slot; explain this kindly with an invitation to enter an answer next time. Existing saved XP stays unchanged; a correct delayed answer can earn a star even in the fourth mission of the day when XP is no longer awarded. After the third mission of a day explain `+0 XP` by the reached daily cap; on a date rollback explain it by paused rewards. The streak bonus is labeled separately from plain XP. Opened stars and XP never decrease because of a later error or a missed day; the streak may break. In maintenance mode XP and stars remain, the streak no longer grows.

### Unambiguous prototype rules

- **Set:** factors 0 through 10 inclusive; 66 unique unordered pairs. The id is `min(a,b):max(a,b)`. All pairs are available. Early missions mix simple and hard facts to probe gaps; there is no mandatory sequential pass starting from 0 and 1.
- **Diagnostics:** first launch introduces Orbi with «Начать знакомство» and «Сразу играть». The first action opens the diagnostic; the second skips it directly. The diagnostic contains 10 distinct facts with simple and hard factors; the chosen set is persisted. Answers and hints are shown after the check finishes. It awards no XP or streak and grants no mastery; the results set the review priority. An in-progress diagnostic can also be skipped to start with unknown facts.
- **Mission composition:** up to 10 distinct facts — reserve two slots for new facts while any remain; fill the rest with facts awaiting review, then familiar facts for free practice. If fewer than two new facts exist, the free slots also go to reviews; if few facts are known, the mission is shorter. An error returns in the next mission, not immediately; the queue is frozen at start. All facts checked by diagnostics count as familiar but not yet mastered: wrong ones are available for review immediately, correct ones tomorrow. A diagnostic success does not start the mastery clock.
- **Schedule:** after the first independent success, a review in 1 day; after the following successful planned reviews — in 3, 7, then every 14 days from the answer date. Early practice does not advance a step or postpone a date. An error or «Не знаю» resets the step and schedules the review for tomorrow; extra practice today is allowed. A success after a solution shown today does not advance the schedule.
- **Mastery:** two independent successes (no solution shown earlier that day) on different days at least 7 calendar days apart with no error in between. After an error the count restarts from a new success. After the first confirmation the achievement star stays open forever. A later error, including a possible typo, adds «Пора повторить» and restarts the learning confirmation but never reduces the star count. The parent sees that mark separately from the achievement; a new confirmation cycle clears it without granting a second star. A plain skipped day never cancels the achievement; overdue facts are marked separately as awaiting check.
- **Completion and rewards:** a mission is complete after every card has been answered and the feedback viewed. At least one entered answer makes the mission eligible for XP; a wrong answer counts equally as effort. A mission made entirely of «Не знаю» is saved as complete but earns no XP, extends no streak, pays no streak bonus and consumes no daily reward slot. The first three eligible completed missions bring at most 30 plain XP per local calendar day; a streak bonus of +20 is paid on top of that limit. One eligible mission extends the streak only once per day. On day 31 there is no new bonus. After a break the same milestones are available again in a new streak. An automatically shortened eligible mission earns the same +10 XP; quitting halfway does not. Completion records store eligibility so hints-only sessions remain idempotent. Historical records without that flag continue to count toward their day's cap; previously earned XP is never removed.

**Expedition finish:** after a mission in which the last of the 66 stars opens, show «Экспедиция завершена!» once and switch to maintenance mode. This achievement means every fact has passed a confirmation at least once; it does not promise error-free knowledge forever. The mission's XP and due bonus follow the normal rules, after which the streak stops updating and resetting; its best result stays in the report. Save the transition together with the mission completion so a relaunch does not replay the event.

**Maintenance mode:** the home screen offers reviews when their date is due and shows the nearest date when the queue is empty. Free practice is always available; the daily streak and its bonuses are no longer used. XP for completed missions and levels keep their previous rules. Later errors return facts to review but never cancel the expedition finish. New math topics are not in this version.

These are starting product rules, not a clinical protocol or a proven dosage of practice. Successes on one day after the answer was shown do not confirm long-term retention.

## 4. Requirements for the first version

| ID | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- |
| M1 | First launch offers a friendly, skippable introduction to Orbi, then a short timer-free diagnostic giving a baseline knowledge estimate over a sample of facts from the 0–10 range. «Сразу играть» skips it directly. Unchecked facts do not count as mastered. Profiles and program settings are not needed. |
| M2 | Cards show the expression before the answer. Input accepts integers 0–100; an empty string, a sign, a fraction, or other characters trigger an input hint rather than a learning mistake; «Не знаю» shows the answer and the hint. Seeing the solution after a wrong or «Не знаю» attempt does not mark the fact mastered; a later independent correct answer can still open its star under the mastery rule. `7×8` and `8×7` count as one fact, though they may appear in both orders. |
| M3 | A mission has no more than 10 cards. Examples after an error return later; planned independent successes get their next review date from section 3. Skipping sessions does not erase learning progress. |
| M4 | XP, levels, streak, and bonuses follow the table above. A relaunch or an interrupted mission never pays a reward twice; an unfinished mission keeps the attempts already made. |
| M5 | The first star requires two independent correct answers on different days at least 7 days apart with no error between them. The screen shows **XP**, **opened stars**, and **facts due for review** separately; an error never reduces the stars. |
| M6 | The PWA installs from Safari to the Home Screen and, after the «Готово без интернета» message, launches the cards offline, including after being closed. Progress survives a reload. Large buttons and digits are comfortable in portrait on the iPhone 12 Pro Max; no sound is needed. If local storage is unavailable, the app warns about it. |
| M7 | After the 66th star is earned and the mission completed the expedition finishes once. Maintenance mode survives a relaunch; the daily streak is off, reviews and free practice are available, and a later error does not cancel the achievement. |

**Saving:** one versioned `localStorage` snapshot contains facts, attempts, the current mission, and the awards. Save every accepted attempt; save the mission completion and its reward as one update carrying the mission id. A repeated tap and a recovery after closing never duplicate attempts or XP. On a write failure never claim a successful save: offer a retry or continue with an explicit «Прогресс не сохраняется» mark. Never clear corrupt or unknown data versions silently; show a message and a separate confirmed reset action.

**Dates:** the schedule uses the local calendar date of the answer; XP and the streak use the date of mission completion, including across midnight. Compare calendar days, not 24-hour intervals. Several missions in one day do not extend the streak twice. If the device date becomes earlier than the last rewarded date, practice stays available but XP and streak changes are paused until the date recovers; stars still depend only on the dates of independent answers. Verify time-zone changes; robust protection against clock tampering is out of scope.

**PWA and data:** static HTTPS hosting, first load from the network, and successful caching of every required resource. Check offline launch of the installed PWA specifically. Offer app updates between missions; never reload a lesson automatically. Storage is tied to the browser context and the site address; do not promise a transfer from Safari into the installed PWA or to a different address. Clearing data, deleting the app, or browser eviction may lose progress; persistence is not guaranteed by [WebKit's storage policy](https://webkit.org/blog/14403/updates-to-storage-policy/). The report needs a short note about the absence of a backup. Export, sync, and protection against local data modification are out of scope for now.

## 5. How to know it helps

Before starting, run a short check without hints and without a timer; repeat the persisted diagnostic set after 2 and 4 weeks, showing solutions only after the check. This is home observation, not an experiment proving causality. **Primary outcome:** the share of correct first answers without a hint among checked facts that have not been shown for the last ≥7 days. Show the numerator and the denominator; if there are no such facts, show «Пока нет данных». This retention outcome differs from cumulative mastery status and diagnostic accuracy. Additionally watch whether he wants to return to missions and whether he can solve a simple everyday multiplication problem. 🔶 **Working hypothesis:** over four weeks the share of retained facts grows noticeably; set a numeric target after the baseline diagnostic. If the game causes stress or the child starts playing for points, change the game rules instead of strengthening the rewards.

## 6. Screen time — a separate decision after checking the cards

If the link to entertainment is still needed, the PWA can show **proposed** minutes; the parent approves the extra time manually through [Apple Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/guide/child-safety/approve-childs-purchases-downloads-requests-dq4vly6fq1je/web). This is a family agreement, not a system lock. Before building any counter, check that the PWA remains available while restrictions are active on the phone. Automatically managing other apps requires the native [Screen Time API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screentimeapidocumentation/) and is not part of this project.

## 7. Prototype decisions and verification

- 🔵 **Open question:** which facts does the son know now, and does he understand multiplication as equal groups? The cards will give a baseline accuracy; understanding of equal groups the parent checks separately with an everyday question.
- **Decided:** once all 66 stars are opened the expedition finishes and moves to maintenance mode without a daily streak; no new topics are added.
- **Decided:** the game theme is the Orbit Club space expedition with the original companion Orbi; the cards remain the main element of the lesson. Colors, typography, components, and reduced-motion behavior are defined in `DESIGN.md`.

**Next step:** try the playable PWA with the son and verify the baseline diagnostic, then refine the cosmic expedition presentation without changing the learning rules. Tune mission length and rewards only if home observation warrants it; add other mechanics only on explicit need.

**Device check:** record the actual iOS version and verify installation, an airplane-mode launch, mission recovery, keyboard input, text zoom, and a PWA update without losing progress. The phone model alone does not confirm compatibility.
